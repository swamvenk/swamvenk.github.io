Case — Jonsbo D33 Black Wood

Not on any in‑stock list. Overseas ~US$99–117 / RM 429 → expect ~$140–180 in SG; ask for a pre‑order. TechDeals carries Jonsbo (D41 on their list) and can order the D33 Wood in. Fallback today: Jonsbo D41 Mesh (wood front) ~$73–78.

D33 price is an estimate until a pre‑order quote lands.

Aesthetic — wood accents + fireplace glow

  • Keep the ProArt 5070 Ti's dark-metal/minimalist line visible through the glass next to the wood front.
  • Set every RGB to warm amber → deep orange (no cold blue/white) — RAM, fans & GPU read as a glowing hearth.
  • Arctic P12 Pro PST LN is near‑silent (2000‑RPM cap, 0‑rpm under ~5% PWM) with high static pressure — ideal for the D33 front mesh.
  • AK620 G2 is black/no‑RGB — fine under the tinted glass.

Negotiation & call-outs

  • Bundle, then haggle: bring PC Themes' $1,035 split (and TechDeals' $801 ASRock bundle) to walk Dynacore's $898 down.
  • GPU lever: ProArt is firmest at PC Themes $1,649 — counter with Dynacore's $1,819 if they flinch.
  • One‑counter bundle: SSD + cooler + fans at Bizgram in one sit; ask about the AK620 wood cap while there.
  • Pay cash / PayNow at all three.
  • Read the box: RAM = CL30 (not CL36) · T500 = 2TB DRAM · GPU = 16G GDDR7 label · fans = the LN (2000‑RPM) variant.
